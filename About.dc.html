About

Builders of investable companies.

Aveyroni Partners is a global, vertical-agnostic operating partner. We source proprietary deal flow and run portfolio value creation for private equity, family offices and venture capital — and we prepare founder-led companies to become institutionally investable before they raise or exit.

Why we exist

Investment banks execute deals that are already ready. PE and VC operating teams arrive only after capital enters. Aveyroni works in the gap between the two — building the institution, the data room, and the buyer map before either shows up.

We do not specialize by sector or geography. The same catalyst-to-execution method runs wherever a demand/supply gap exists, for as long as the fit between a founder's business and an investor's buy-box needs building rather than finding.
